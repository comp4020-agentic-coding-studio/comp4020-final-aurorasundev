## G01 — desktop orbit, mineral direction

```text
Use case: ui-mockup. Generate ONE complete, highly polished desktop website application screenshot, wide 16:10 landscape around 1680x1050. It is a new design concept for Throwaway, an anonymous paper-burning ritual website, and its public Gallery of AI-generated images and short music shaped by the content of destroyed notes. This is an exhibition browser, not a marketing page or product dashboard. Creative direction: a tactile editorial record exhibition; same world as real wrinkled paper, ash, soft daylight and mineral-grey room, but sophisticated record-disc interaction inspired by experimental portfolios. Background flat-light foggy sage/mineral grey #c6cdcc, subtle soft film grain. Type charcoal #292927; serif Georgia-like wordmark/headings, clean understated sans-serif controls. Very generous negative space and refined precise composition. Entire viewport visible, not a crop. Top left 'Throwaway' serif 40 px, top right 'Back to the space' and 'About', restrained navigation. Upper left below header heading 'The Gallery' in 45 px serif and small supporting line 'Images and sounds shaped by released notes.' No badge or pretitle. Main central exhibition at x=53%,y=54%: an elegant thin charcoal-black vinyl record, 350px diameter, front face tilted only 12 degrees in 3D, concentric fine grooves and a tiny centre spindle hole. It has a substantial round printed paper label showing the SELECTED unique generated artwork: a pale folded-paper ribbon spanning two slate-blue stone-like forms, almost meeting but interrupted, on mist-blue ground; abstract metaphor for an invented sample note about an unfinished conversation; no lettering in the artwork. The record is tactile, not a glossy chrome sphere. Surrounding this central disc is a CLEAR DIAGONALLY TILTED ELLIPTICAL ORBIT of SEVEN distinct square album sleeves/image planes. Ring slopes from upper left to lower right. Larger foreground squares 180-210px, smaller distant squares 100-130px, mild 3D angles, soft restrained contact/ambient shadows, no visible orbit lines or globe. All are visibly separate artworks with generous breathing room and never overlap header/text/centre image: sage thread knot; smoky glass vessel holding a tiny light; misty open threshold; two charcoal pebbles drifting apart; pale paper arch; muted cobalt ribbon unfurling; single seed in a soft ivory void. Same cohesive visual style across covers: abstract material collage, scanned paper fibres, subdued risograph/halftone, low-fi dimensional still-life, quiet muted sage/blue/ivory/charcoal, tiny faded terracotta accent on one image. Each picture represents different invented note content; no stock repeated artwork. Keep original artwork SQUARE on sleeve planes, do not crop it into tiny record labels on all satellite items. Bottom left selected work name 'An unfinished thread' in 26px serif and subline 'The original note is gone.' Bottom centre 'Scroll to turn · Click to open' in 15px sans. Bottom right plain left/right arrow controls with 44px tap areas, minimal styling. No visible play-progress bar in this browsing state; no automatic music, no 'Now playing' label. This should look premium, fashion-editorial, gently experimental and buildable with Three.js and ordinary HTML typography. Avoid generic cards, giant rounded panels, bento layouts, sidebars, fake metrics, profile avatars, likes, dates, source note text, sci-fi HUD, shiny plastic, loud neon, game graphics, thick shadows, illegible microtext, browser chrome. All UI text and controls are clearly legible and would be implemented in HTML, never baked into runtime artwork. This image is a visual reference only.
```
